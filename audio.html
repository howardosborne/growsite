John Bunyan and the Pilgrim's Progress

Between Pavenham and Bromham Mill, the Ouse Valley Way joins another walking route - The John Bunyan Trail. It's an 86 mile loop that seeks to follow in the footsteps of John Bunyan who used to walk these parts as a tinker and preacher. That was before being put in prison and then using his time doing stir to write The Pilgrim's Progress. I met with John Pestell from the Bunyan Museum to find out more about man and his greatest work. Bunyan Museum
